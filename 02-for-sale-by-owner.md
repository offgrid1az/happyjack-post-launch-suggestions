## The query

Autocomplete, 24 November 2026 — the FSBO variant appears under **five separate seeds**:

```
happy jack az homes for sale by owner        (seed: happy jack az homes for sale)
happy jack land for sale by owner            (seed: happy jack land)
pine az homes for sale by owner              (seed: pine az homes for sale)
strawberry az homes for sale by owner        (seed: strawberry az homes for sale)
land for sale happy jack az no hoa by owner  (seed: happy jack az land for sale)
```

That repetition is the signal: "by owner" is not an occasional modifier, it is a standard way
people search this market. On the Rim the reason is understandable — a lot of property here is
sold between families, neighbours and long-time owners, often without ever being listed.

## What the site says today

- "for sale by owner" — **0 of 14** pages
- "FSBO" — **1 of 14**, on `/guides` only, as a passing mention

The four search pages that would naturally answer it say "search our listings" and link out. They
never address the person who is searching *because* they do not want an agent involved, or who is
an owner thinking about selling without one.

## What to add

A short section on each search page — three or four sentences, no new page needed.

> **Buying or selling without an agent in Happy Jack**
>
> Plenty of Rim property changes hands between neighbours and families before it is ever listed,
> and some of what you see on the portals is owner-listed. If you are buying, we can tell you what
> an owner-listed parcel is actually worth here and what the due diligence it skips usually costs.
> If you are selling on your own, we will tell you what your cabin or lot is worth — no obligation,
> and no pitch.

That last half is the important one. The high-intent searcher for "by owner" is as often a
**seller** as a buyer, and a seller who is already thinking about going it alone is a conversation
worth having early, before they list badly and lose a season.

Also worth adding to the same section: what an owner-sale skips in this market specifically —
title work on old parcels, access and easement verification, water well documentation, septic
records. Those are the things that stall a private sale on the Rim, and naming them is
demonstrated expertise rather than a sales line.
